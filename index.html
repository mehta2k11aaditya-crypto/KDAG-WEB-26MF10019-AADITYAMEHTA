About Us

KDAG (Kharagpur Data Analytics Group) is a student-run community dedicated to helping beginners break into the world of Python, data analytics and machine learning. Through this bootcamp, we aim to build a strong foundation for anyone curious about data - no prior experience required, just enthusiasm to learn.
